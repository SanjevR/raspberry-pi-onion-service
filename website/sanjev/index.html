Hi, I'm Sanjev

I built this Onion site using a Raspberry Pi, Linux, Tor, Nginx, local HTML and CSS, locally hosted images, OpenStreetMap-derived data, and a lot of testing, retesting, troubleshooting, and rebuilding.

I believe in privacy, encryption, security, freedom of information, and giving people more control over how they access the Internet.

I Grew Up With the Evolution of Technology

Windows 95, 98, 2000, and XP
I grew up using the early generations of Windows computers.

Floppy disks, CD-ROMs, and USB drives
I watched portable storage become smaller, faster, and easier to use.

Dial-up to broadband
I remember when getting online meant listening to a modem connect.

Desktop computers to laptops
Computing gradually became more portable and powerful.

Flip phones to smartphones
Phones became cameras, navigation systems, entertainment devices, and pocket computers.

Traditional websites to social media and AI
In 2026, I still experiment, learn, and keep up with new technology.
